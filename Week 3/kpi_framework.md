# Week 3 – Day 5: KPI Framework

## Business Domain: Retail Sales

### Objective
Monitor sales performance, customer activity, and operational efficiency
using meaningful Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).

| No. | KPI | Formula | Owner | Frequency | Target | Type |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Total Revenue | Sum of Sales Revenue | Sales Manager | Monthly | ₹150,000 | Lagging |
| 2 | Total Sales Quantity | Sum of Products Sold | Sales Manager | Weekly | 100 units | Lagging |
| 3 | Average Order Value | Total Revenue / Number of Orders | Business Analyst | Monthly | ₹10,000 | Lagging |
| 4 | Customer Retention Rate | Returning Customers / Total Customers × 100 | Marketing Manager | Monthly | 70% | Lagging |
| 5 | Customer Acquisition Rate | New Customers / Total Customers × 100 | Marketing Manager | Monthly | 20% | Leading |
| 6 | Discount Rate | Discounted Orders / Total Orders × 100 | Sales Manager | Weekly | Below 15% | Leading |
| 7 | Product Demand | Quantity Sold per Product | Inventory Manager | Weekly | Monitor Growth | Leading |
| 8 | Customer Satisfaction Score | Total Satisfaction Ratings / Number of Responses | Customer Success Manager | Monthly | 4.5/5 | Leading |
| 9 | Inventory Availability | Available Products / Total Products × 100 | Inventory Manager | Daily | 95% | Leading |
| 10 | Sales Growth Rate | (Current Revenue - Previous Revenue) / Previous Revenue × 100 | Finance Manager | Monthly | 10% | Lagging |

## Leading vs Lagging Indicators

### Leading Indicators
Leading indicators help predict future business performance.

Examples:
- Customer Acquisition Rate
- Discount Rate
- Product Demand
- Customer Satisfaction Score
- Inventory Availability

### Lagging Indicators
Lagging indicators measure results after an activity has occurred.

Examples:
- Total Revenue
- Total Sales Quantity
- Average Order Value
- Customer Retention Rate
- Sales Growth Rate

## KPI Target Setting

KPI targets should be:
- Specific
- Measurable
- Achievable
- Relevant
- Time-bound (SMART)

Targets should be based on historical performance, business goals,
industry benchmarks, and available resources.
