# KPI Dashboard Narrative

## Business Domain: Retail Sales

### Overview
The KPI dashboard provides an overview of retail sales performance
using revenue, quantity, average price, and discount metrics.

### Key Observations
1. Total sales revenue provides an overall view of business performance.
2. Sales by city helps identify geographical sales distribution.
3. The revenue trend chart helps monitor sales changes over time.
4. Category-wise sales comparison highlights product category performance.
5. Category and date slicers allow users to explore the data interactively.

### Trends and Anomalies
The dashboard can be used to identify increases or decreases in revenue,
variations across cities, and differences in category performance.

The available dataset is limited, so long-term trends and significant
anomalies require additional historical data.

### Business Recommendations
- Monitor revenue trends regularly.
- Identify high-performing product categories.
- Compare sales performance across cities.
- Review discounts and their relationship with revenue.
- Use KPI targets to track future performance.

### Conclusion
The KPI dashboard provides a simple and interactive way to monitor
retail sales performance and support data-driven decision-making.
