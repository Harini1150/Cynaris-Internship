# Week 4 — Day 2: Probability

## Objective
Applied probability concepts and probability distributions using Python for data analysis and business forecasting.

## Practical Tasks Completed

### 1. Basic Probability
Created a contingency table using customer response data.

- P(Responded) = 0.70
- P(Existing AND Responded) = 0.40
- P(Responded | Existing) = 0.80

### 2. Normal Distribution
Created a normal distribution plot using a mean of 100 and standard deviation of 15.

Applied the 68-95-99.7 rule:
- Within 1 standard deviation: approximately 68%
- Within 2 standard deviations: approximately 95%
- Within 3 standard deviations: approximately 99.7%

### 3. Binomial Distribution
Simulated 1,000 trials where:
- Number of trials = 10
- Probability of success = 60%

Average simulated purchases = 5.996, which is approximately 6 purchases.

### 4. Central Limit Theorem
Performed a sampling simulation using an exponential population.

- Population mean = 48.875
- Mean of sample means = 49.192

The two values are close, demonstrating how sample means tend to center around the population mean.

## Key Insights
- Conditional probability helps measure the likelihood of an event given another condition.
- Normal distributions are useful for understanding naturally varying measurements.
- Binomial distributions model repeated independent success/failure outcomes.
- The Central Limit Theorem explains why sample means tend toward a normal distribution as sample size increases.
