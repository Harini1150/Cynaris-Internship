# Week 4 — Day 1: Descriptive Statistics

## Objective
Applied descriptive statistics to summarize and interpret a retail sales dataset.

## Dataset
Cleaned sales dataset containing 9 records and 9 columns.

## Statistical Measures
- Mean
- Median
- Mode
- Variance
- Standard Deviation
- Percentiles and Quartiles

## Visualizations
- Price frequency distribution histogram
- Price skewness analysis
- Price percentiles
- Category-wise price box plot

## Key Insights
1. The price distribution is positively skewed.
2. Price skewness is approximately 1.304.
3. The median price is ₹6,000.
4. The third quartile (Q3) is ₹15,000.
5. Quantity has a mode of 1.
6. Discount has a mode of 10%.
7. Price variation is high across the dataset.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Conclusion
Descriptive statistics helped summarize the dataset and identify price distribution patterns, variability, and differences across product categories.
